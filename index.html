ABOUT ADHIMUKTI TRANS || AGEN WISATA BROMO, IJEN & TUMPAK SEWU

Extreme terrain experience with complete comfort.

We started as a community of local drivers from Probolinggo, Lumajang, and Banyuwangi with an in-depth understanding of the terrain and natural conditions of Mount Bromo, Ijen Crater, and Tumpak Sewu Waterfall.

Our 4x4 jeep fleet and troop transports are regularly maintained to ensure your safety while crossing the Bromo Sea of Sand, navigating the lush valleys of Tumpak Sewu, and conquering the steep climbs of Paltuding Ijen.

OUR SERVICES

Designed for your travel flexibility.

From station and airport transfers to private jeep rentals and dedicated waterfall explorations across East Java.

Private Bromo Jeep Rental

Pickup from Sukapura, Cemoro Lawang, or Malang. Includes Penanjakan Sunrise, Widodaren, Bromo Crater, Whispering Sands, and Teletubbies Savanna.

Ijen Crater Blue Fire Tour

Midnight pickup from Banyuwangi or Bondowoso. Includes a licensed local guide, standard respirator gas mask, and entrance ticket.

Tumpak Sewu Waterfall Tour

Private day trip from Malang, Surabaya, or Lumajang. Explore the majestic panoramic view at Panorama Cliff, trek down to the bottom of the waterfall, and visit Goa Tetes.
